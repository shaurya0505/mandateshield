# MandateShield: Evaluation Framework & Counterfactual Benchmarking

## 1. Executive Summary

MandateShield employs a **Paired Counterfactual Simulation** methodology to evaluate recurring revenue recovery performance. 

Rather than generating disparate datasets or making unfounded claims about production lift, MandateShield clones every simulated payment failure scenario across **three distinct deterministic policies** in parallel under identical seeds.

---

## 2. Evaluation Policies

| Policy | Behavior & Assumptions | Implementation Details |
|---|---|---|
| **`NO_RECOVERY`** | **Zero Intervention Baseline**<br>Observes the initial debit failure, records exposure, and performs zero retries or notifications. | Case remains in `RECOVERY_ELIGIBLE` with 0 recovered revenue and 0 operational costs. |
| **`FIXED_RETRY`** | **Deterministic Fixed-Retry Baseline**<br>Re-attempts debits on the primary mandate at fixed 24-hour intervals up to a maximum of 3 retries. Blind to historical payment timing, rail degradation, and alternate mandates. | Respects basic provider rules (e.g. stops on revoked mandates), incurs simulated debit fees (₹5.00/attempt), but lacks contextual intelligence. |
| **`MANDATESHIELD`** | **Context-Aware Intelligent Recovery**<br>Evaluates factual context envelope, generates bounded strategy proposal, enforces deterministic Policy Guardian safety checks, executes via authorized executor, and reconciles asynchronously. | Employs `WAIT_AND_RETRY` (historical payment window timing), `SWITCH_PAYMENT_PATH` (rail outage bypass), `STOP_RECOVERY` (revoked mandates), and `ESCALATE_TO_HUMAN`. |

## 3. Core Metric Definitions

All financial calculations use strict **integer paisa arithmetic** (₹1 = 100 paisa) to prevent floating-point drift.

### A. Gross Recovery
$$\text{Gross Recovery (paisa)} = \sum \text{Successfully Recovered Principal Amount}$$

### B. Net Recovery
$$\text{Net Recovery (paisa)} = \text{Gross Recovery} - \sum \text{Modeled Operational Costs}$$
- Debit attempt cost: ₹5.00 (500 paisa)
- Customer nudge cost: ₹2.00 (200 paisa)
- Payment link cost: ₹1.00 (100 paisa)
- Human escalation review cost: ₹100.00 (10,000 paisa)

### C. Case-Count Recovery Rate
$$\text{Recovery Rate} = \frac{\text{Recovered Cases}}{\text{Eligible Cases}}$$

### D. Value-Weighted Monetary Recovery Rate
$$\text{Value-Weighted Rate} = \frac{\text{Gross Recovered Amount (paisa)}}{\text{Total Revenue at Risk (paisa)}}$$

### E. Customer Contact / Harassment Index
An internal interpretable friction index measuring customer agitation:
$$\text{Contact Index} = (\text{Total Retries} \times 1.0) + (\text{Total Nudges} \times 1.5) + (\text{Escalations} \times 0.5)$$

### F. Unnecessary Retries
A retry attempt is deterministically flagged as **unnecessary** if:
1. Attempted on a revoked / inactive mandate.
2. Attempted during an active payment rail outage without switching paths.
3. Attempted outside the customer's historical payment window on an insufficient funds failure (where an intelligent wait would have avoided the wasted debit and fee).
4. Attempted after the case was already in a terminal state.

### G. Comparative Lift
- **Simulated Gross Lift vs Fixed Retry:** $\text{MandateShield Gross} - \text{Fixed Retry Gross}$
- **Simulated Net Lift vs Fixed Retry:** $\text{MandateShield Net} - \text{Fixed Retry Net}$
- **Percentage Lift:** $\frac{\text{MandateShield Gross} - \text{Fixed Retry Gross}}{\text{Fixed Retry Gross}} \times 100\%$ (Returns `None` if baseline is 0).

## 4. Methodological Invariants & Fintech Clarifications

### Why AI Confidence is NOT Recovery Probability
`StrategyProposal.strategy_confidence` represents the LLM's self-reported confidence in its recommendation (e.g. 0.85). It is **not** a statistically calibrated probability of debt recovery, and is **never** used in revenue forecasting or policy bypasses.

### Synthetic Lift vs Causal Claims
- Results generated by `BatchEvaluator` represent **simulated counterfactual differences** within the deterministic testbed.
- They are **not** real-world randomized control trials (RCTs) or causal econometric estimates.
- They prove that **under realistic synthetic payment frictions (liquidity cycles, rail outages, revoked tokens)**, context-aware recovery mathematically outperforms blind retries while reducing customer harassment.
